# Mole in a hole

__Description:__ 
<br>
Made for the CAMPFIRE 2026 game jam. 
<br>
4th place in the enjoyment category for the EMBER 2026 game jam.

Mole in a hole is a pixel action platform game. Play as a mole who one day wakes up and finds that his world has become a barren waste land. What's worse, there's poisonous fog spreading, meaning that his old home isn't safe. Adventure deeper and deeper under the surface of the earth to escape the poison, avoiding dangers and collecting shiny gems along the way!
You can play it here!: https://lima6.itch.io/mole-in-a-hole 

__Controls:__  
- Arrow keys / AD to move
- Up arrow / W / Space to jump
- Press SPACE to continue the dialogue
- Press X to slash blocks or enemies
- Press Z to interact / save (when near checkpoint)
- ESC to pause the game
<br>

__Features:__  
- Different explorable zones
- 3 different difficulty levels
- Intro and ending cutscenes
- Multiple enemies and obstacles.
